# Video Multimedia AI: Teknologi & AI in Motion

## Overview

A 5 minute 30 second motion-graphics video on the theme "Teknologi dan AI" for a Multimedia practicum — 12 scenes with Indonesian narration (611 words), a title and name/NIM/class footer on every scene, and a music bed. It is 1280x720, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC.

## What the Video Shows

- **12 scenes**: Each scene covers one idea about technology and AI, with flat-vector art and a moving character or object.
- **Characters that move**: A boy walks across the scene with stepping legs, stops, and raises his phone; sound waves pulse; the map pin and route animate.
- **Kinetic text**: Key words move in sync with the narration instead of sitting still.

## Why I Built It This Way

Camera movement over a still image is not animation — only the characters themselves moving counts. So every scene is a pure HTML/SVG puppet: characters and objects are built from shapes, cut into parts, and animated frame by frame (679 frames for the demo scene alone) with easing on every walk cycle.

## Tech Stack

- **HTML/SVG**: Every character and object is hand-built SVG, animated with code.
- **Remotion-style rendering**: Scenes render frame by frame, then assemble into the final MP4.
- **Narration + music**: 12 Indonesian narration tracks mixed with a music bed.

## Building Experience

### The Style Loop

- The first versions were rejected for looking like a slideshow — camera moves, but the images themselves did not move. A single-scene demo (22.6 seconds, the boy walking and raising his phone) was approved first, and only then were all 12 scenes rebuilt in that style.
- The rule for every rebuild since: validate the style on one scene and get a verdict before rebuilding the full video.

### Rendering

- Scenes are rendered frame by frame from HTML/SVG, then assembled with narration and music into the final 326-second video.

## Impact & Results

- **Delivered**: The full 12-scene video (25.6 MB) plus a 13-page report in Word and PDF.
- **Approved style**: The puppet demo scene set the bar for the whole video.

## Future Enhancements

- **Smoother walk cycles**: Wider steps and better easing, the way the Odyssey wolf technique was refined.
- **More scenes**: The same pipeline can carry longer videos.

## Key Learnings

- **Validate one scene first**: Never rebuild 12 scenes on an unapproved style.
- **Characters must move**: Camera motion alone is not animation.
- **Easing is everything**: The same walk looks stiff or alive depending on the easing curve.
